# AI-Powered Quality Control — Lead Generation

**Nexus Contact Center | Quality & Transformation Project**

Quality control transformation for Lead Generation — from sample-based manual auditing toward automated, broader coverage, supported by AI experimentation, validation and CRM integration.

> **Confidentiality:** This case is intentionally anonymized. Client names, individual lead data, proprietary prompts, detailed CRM architecture and sensitive operational information are excluded.

## Executive Summary

The project addressed a structural quality-control challenge: manual auditing based on samples limited coverage and required significant evaluator effort.

The transformation introduced an **AI-assisted quality-control approach**, progressively moving from business requirements and experimentation to testing, automation and integration into an internal CRM.

**Business Requirements → Process Transformation → AI Experimentation → Testing & Validation → Automation → CRM Integration → Deployment**

## Context & Challenge

The Lead Generation environment required quality control across multiple projects while maintaining consistent evaluation standards.

Key challenges:
- Quality control based primarily on manual sampling
- Limited coverage of the total Lead Generation population
- Significant manual audit effort
- Need for consistent application of quality criteria
- Need to validate AI-generated assessments before operational use
- Need to move from experimentation toward an industrialised solution
- Need to connect quality control with the existing operational CRM environment

The objective was not simply to introduce an AI tool, but to **transform the quality-control process itself**.

## Business Requirements & Project Framing

The project started with a **Business Requirements Document (BRD)** to formalise the business need and establish the framework for the transformation.

The BRD structured the business context, objectives, scope, quality requirements, evaluation criteria, constraints, success criteria and stakeholder expectations.

### Project objective

Move from a **sample-based quality-control model** toward a solution capable of supporting **100% control coverage**, while preserving quality standards, validation mechanisms and operational usability.

## Process Transformation

### AS-IS

**Lead / Interaction → Sample Selection → Manual Audit → Evaluation → Consolidation → Reporting**

### TO-BE

**Lead / Interaction → Automated Processing → Quality Assessment → Validation / Control → CRM → Reporting**

The transformation addressed both the **control mechanism** and the surrounding process. Automation was designed around defined quality criteria rather than replacing the quality methodology.

## AI Experimentation & POC

The project moved through an experimentation phase to determine how AI could support the quality-control requirements.

The experimentation focused on automated analysis, application of predefined quality criteria, assessment consistency, identification of gaps, comparison with human evaluation and iterative refinement before deployment.

The objective was to **validate business value and reliability before industrialisation**.

## Testing & Validation

**Test → Review → Identify Gaps → Adjust → Retest → Validate**

Validation covered assessment reliability, alignment with quality criteria, consistency, discrepancies, operational usability and readiness for broader deployment.

Human quality expertise remained part of the validation and control mechanism.

## Automation & CRM Integration

The solution evolved from **standalone AI experimentation → automated quality-control logic → dedicated quality-control module → internal CRM integration**.

My contribution was primarily on the **business, quality and transformation side**: requirements, process logic, quality criteria, testing, validation, stakeholder coordination and operational alignment.

Technical implementation was handled by the relevant technical resources.

## KPI Framework

| KPI | Objective / Evidence |
|---|---:|
| **Control coverage** | **100% target** |
| **Automated quality assessment reliability / alignment** | **≈85%** |
| **Manual audit effort** | **≈60% reduction** |
| **Deployment scope** | Lead Generation projects |
| **Integration level** | Internal CRM quality-control module |

### KPI Definitions

- **Control coverage:** proportion of the targeted Lead Generation population subject to automated quality control.
- **Automated assessment reliability / alignment:** observed level of alignment between automated assessment and applicable quality-control criteria / reference evaluation.
- **Manual audit effort:** reduction in human effort required for routine quality-control auditing.
- **Deployment scope:** progressive extension across Lead Generation projects.

Detailed calculation methodologies and underlying operational datasets remain confidential.

## Results

- **100% control coverage target**
- **≈85% automated assessment reliability / alignment**
- **≈60% reduction in manual audit effort**
- Progressive industrialisation to **CRM-integrated quality control**
- Broader deployment capability across Lead Generation projects

The results reflect the combination of **quality framework design, process transformation, AI experimentation, validation and operational integration**.
