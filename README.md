# AI-Powered Quality Control — Lead Generation

Quality control transformation for Lead Generation — AI-assisted automation, 100% coverage, and CRM integration.

## Project focus

A Quality & Transformation project designed to evolve Lead Generation quality control from sample-based manual auditing toward broader automated coverage, while preserving quality standards and human validation.

## Approach

**BRD → Process Analysis → AI POC → Testing & Validation → Automation → CRM Integration → Deployment**

## Key outcomes

- **100% control coverage target** across Lead Generation projects
- **≈85% reliability/alignment** of automated quality assessment
- **≈40% reduction** in manual audit effort
- Progressive transition from AI experimentation to a dedicated quality-control module integrated into the internal CRM

## Contribution

- Business requirements and project framing
- Quality-control process analysis and transformation
- Definition and translation of quality criteria into an AI-assisted control approach
- AI experimentation and proof of concept
- Testing, validation and continuous improvement
- Coordination between quality, operational and technical stakeholders
- Support for automation and CRM integration
- Deployment across Lead Generation projects

## Methodology

The project followed a progressive transformation path:

1. **Business framing** — formalization of the need through a BRD
2. **Process analysis** — review of the existing quality-control model and definition of the target process
3. **AI experimentation** — evaluation of AI-assisted approaches against operational and quality requirements
4. **Testing & validation** — review of outputs, identification of gaps and refinement
5. **Automation** — transition from experimentation toward an automated quality-control process
6. **CRM integration** — evolution toward a dedicated internal CRM module
7. **Deployment** — extension across Lead Generation projects

## Confidentiality
